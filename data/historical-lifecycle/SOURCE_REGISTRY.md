## Entry rules

- Exact action date needs direct source statement or transparent exact-date derivation.
- Founding-anchor precision must match source. Never use year/month anchor as exact day.
- Historical primary source mode requires reviewed lifecycle episode and action.
- Year/month-only founding anchors never produce an exact age. They may support a bounded age range only when action date is exact and entire range fits selected stage band.


## Product milestone research

The product-milestones.json manifest retains reviewed milestone dates separately from company founding dates. The episode importer accepts --milestones and attaches these records to their product episodes. Business-event API responses expose product_milestones dated on or before the event action, including days_before_action. Founder recommendation selection and UI support for product scope remain pending. Public announcement, unveiling, retail release and production start are different milestones; none is automatically a product development inception date.
